## Unfocused Music Effect

If you don't like how loud your background music is while Geometry Dash is minimized or out of focus, this mod can help!

There are two customization options to control how your background music behaves when the game is not in focus:

- Volume
- Low-pass filter
- Fade transition when switching focus
